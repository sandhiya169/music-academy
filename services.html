Piano Program

Hand Posture & Scales

The foundation of all our piano programs, ensuring injury-free play and long-term dexterity. Students build muscle memory through structured daily scale routines before advancing to repertoire.

  • Wrist & arm alignment
  • Major & minor scale fluency
  • Sight-reading introduction
Book a trial lesson
Vocal Arts

Vocal Warmups & Breath

Mastering the diaphragm to support sustained notes without straining vocal cords. Every session begins with a structured 15-minute warmup protocol tailored to the student's voice type.

  • Diaphragmatic breathing
  • Resonance placement
  • Register & passaggio
Book a trial lesson
Ensemble

Chamber Ensemble Rehearsal

Learning to listen and synchronize within a quartet environment. Students discover ensemble dynamics, balance, and how individual parts serve the collective musical statement.

  • Intonation & blend
  • Score reading & cues
  • Performance preparation
Book a trial lesson
Production

Studio Mixing Basics

An introduction to EQ, compression, and spatial awareness in the control room. Students work on real project files in our industry-standard DAW environment from day one.

  • EQ & frequency balance
  • Dynamic compression
  • Reverb & stereo field
